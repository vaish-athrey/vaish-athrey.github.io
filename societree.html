Societree Volunteering

Four community campaigns I volunteered on with Societree, covering public health, education, hygiene access, and food insecurity.

Mask Up India

This initiative raised awareness about wearing masks to prevent the spread of COVID-19, especially in underserved areas. Volunteers distributed masks and educated communities on proper mask usage, hygiene practices, and social distancing to keep people safe during the pandemic.

Enlighten India

This event focused on education and awareness around social issues such as child labor, education for all, and women's empowerment. Volunteers organized workshops, seminars, and campaigns to spread knowledge and encourage community involvement in creating positive change.

The Period Society

The Pads Bank initiative provided sanitary products to women and girls in underprivileged communities who lacked access to menstrual hygiene products. The goal was to tackle menstrual health stigma, promote awareness, and make sure every woman had access to the hygiene products she needed.

Hunger Free India

This campaign addressed food insecurity by providing meals to people in marginalized communities. Volunteers organized food drives, distributed essential supplies, and raised awareness about the growing hunger crisis in India during a period of economic hardship.

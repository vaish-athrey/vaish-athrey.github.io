Societree Website

A feature-rich website built to close the gap between volunteers and NGOs, with applications, opportunity listings, and a sustainable-products marketplace.

What it does

Societree connects volunteers with NGOs. The site lets volunteers apply directly to opportunities and get updates tailored to each NGO's specific needs, so matching isn't left to a generic listings page. I designed and built the site around those two groups of users: volunteers looking for opportunities, and NGOs trying to reach them.

Beyond volunteer matching

The platform also includes an online shopping portal that lets NGOs sell sustainable products directly, giving them an additional revenue stream beyond donations. Regular updates on events and drives keep the community informed in something close to real time, which was part of the goal: make the platform feel active rather than static.

Outcome

The finished site gave Societree a single place to run volunteer applications, publish opportunities, sell NGO products, and post updates, streamlining work that had previously been scattered across manual processes. It's a small example of using ordinary web development to support a social organization's actual operations rather than just giving it a homepage.

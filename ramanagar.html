Volunteering Education

Ramanagar Program

Building hands-on educational content for 3rd-grade Environmental Science students.

What we built

As part of the Ramanagar Program, our team worked on enhancing the educational experience for 3rd-grade students studying Environmental Science. We developed video content with voiceovers based on the prescribed EVS curriculum, and created detailed charts on various topics as a lasting resource for current and future students.

We also designed interactive PowerPoint presentations to simplify complex concepts, plus AR-VR videos showcasing plant varieties, built as conversation-style content to make the material more relatable, including how it connects to a balanced diet.

The greenhouse model

The highlight of the project was a working model of a greenhouse management system, used to visually demonstrate why greenhouses matter for environmental sustainability. We paired it with an educational video explaining greenhouse gases and their significance, giving students a hands-on, interactive way to learn about their environment.

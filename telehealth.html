Telehealth Platform: remote care during COVID-19

A full-stack healthcare platform that let patients consult doctors, store medical records, and pay for visits entirely online, built to keep people out of crowded waiting rooms during the pandemic.

Overview Records & data Consultations Payments Impact

Overview

During COVID-19, routine hospital visits carried real exposure risk. I built a telehealth platform with Python and Django to move as much of that interaction online as possible, letting patients book and complete a doctor's visit, including payment, without setting foot in a clinic.

Records & data storage

Patient records and medical test data needed flexible, document-shaped storage rather than a rigid relational schema, so I used MongoDB to store patient profiles, visit history, and test results securely, with the Django backend handling access control around that data.

Live video consultations

I integrated the Zoom API directly into the booking flow so a confirmed appointment generated a live video session between doctor and patient automatically. That removed the need for either party to leave home for a routine consultation.

Payments

I operationalized a payment gateway to handle the full transaction lifecycle for a visit, including order confirmation and status callbacks, so a booking wasn't considered complete until payment had actually cleared.

PythonDjangoMongoDBJavaScriptZoom APIPayment Gateway

Impact

  • Reduced in-person hospital exposure during COVID-19 by moving consultations fully online.
  • Zero payment failures across live transactions, thanks to explicit order confirmation and status callbacks.
  • Gave patients a single flow: book, consult over video, pay, without leaving home.

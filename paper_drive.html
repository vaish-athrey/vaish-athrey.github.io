Volunteering Rotaract RVCE

Rotaract Paper Drive

A community-driven recycling initiative focused on collecting recyclable paper across Bangalore neighborhoods.

What it was

The Rotaract RVCE Paper Drive promoted recycling and environmental awareness. Volunteers went door to door across various localities in Bangalore, reaching households, schools, and businesses to collect recyclable paper that would otherwise go to waste.

The drive collected significant amounts of paper, which was sorted and sent to recycling centers. It was a hands-on way to emphasize the importance of recycling while bringing the community together around a shared effort to reduce waste and support sustainable practices.
